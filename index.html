Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Offer Poster Studio ("we", "us") provides a poster-design tool for shops and businesses. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide: email address, password (securely handled by our authentication provider), and shop/business name.
  • Content you create: uploaded product photos, logos, poster text and saved poster drafts.
  • Payment details you submit for plan upgrades: UPI transaction ID and, if provided, a payment screenshot — used only to verify and activate your plan.
  • Basic usage data such as download counts, needed to enforce plan limits.

How we use your information

  • To provide the poster editor, save your drafts, and let you download your posters.
  • To activate and manage your subscription plan.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

Data storage

Your account data, uploaded images and poster drafts are stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Your choices

You can edit your business profile at any time from the app. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us via the Contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to us via our contact page.
